⬇ GrabInsta

Public posts only · No login · No watermark added

Save a public Reel or post, in its original file.

Paste an Instagram link. GrabInsta returns a download link for every photo and video in that post — one per carousel slide. Files open straight from Instagram’s own servers, so nothing large passes through this site.

Works with public Reels, single photos, single videos and carousels. Stories, private accounts and login-only posts are not supported.

Tip: press Enter in the link box to fetch.

Your media

Saving: tap Download — the file opens in a new tab from Instagram’s server. In most browsers, long-press (mobile) or right-click (desktop) the opened file and choose Save. The download button does not force a save because the file lives on another site.

How it works

  1. Copy the link — in Instagram, tap ··· on a public post or Reel, then Copy link.
  2. Paste it here and tap Get download links.
  3. Download each slide — carousels list every photo and video separately.

What it cannot do

  • Stories, private accounts and posts that need a login.
  • Audio extraction or video conversion — you get the file Instagram stored.
  • Guaranteed access: Instagram sometimes blocks server requests (you’ll see an honest “blocked” message, not a fake link). Only download things you own or have permission to save, and respect the creator’s rights.
